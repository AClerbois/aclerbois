## 👨‍💻 About me

I fell in love with computers at the age of 12 and built my first website (with far too many nested tables 😅) at 13. That passion never left — it simply grew into a career.

Today, I'm a **Microsoft MVP** in **Microsoft Foundry (AI) & Developer Technologies**, and the founder and **Senior Technical Architect** of [Sense of Tech](https://www.senseof.tech), my Belgium-based company focused on **software craftsmanship**, **.NET**, **DevOps** (Azure DevOps & GitHub) and **AI**. Through Sense of Tech, I help **medium and large enterprises improve their efficiency** — and I'm increasingly wearing the **Forward Deployed Engineer** hat, working hands-on with teams to turn modern engineering practices and AI into real, shipped value.

Before going independent, I worked as a consultant at Trasys (now part of **KEYES**, formerly the NRB group) on industrial projects, and as an Enterprise Lead Designer at Ingenico, customizing an ePayment platform for international clients such as Carrefour.

Over the years, I've had the chance to work with organizations of all sizes — from local ventures like Salade2Fruit to major players such as **Audi**, **AXA Bank**, **ORES** and **Microsoft**.

What drives me is sharing: conferences, workshops, technical articles… as they say, *"we learn something new every day"* — and I love being part of that, both as a learner and as a teacher.
